Drop real sound files here as `<name>.wav` to replace the synthesized
chiptune renders. Names are the keys of `SFX` and `MUSIC` in
`amormortuorum/core/chiptune.py` (for example `slash.wav`, `victory.wav`,
`dungeon.wav`, `boss.wav`). Files here are packaged with the game and take
priority over the synthesizer.
